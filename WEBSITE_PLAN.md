# Lube Prestigious Sdn Bhd - Website Design & Development Plan

## 1. Project Overview
- **Company:** Lube Prestigious Sdn Bhd (Reg No. 202201026971 / 1472668-U)
- **Tech Stack:** React.js
- **Design Philosophy:** Trustworthy, simple, clean, and highly aesthetic. Tailored for the automotive, industrial, and marine supply chain sectors.
- **Design Constraints:** 
  - ZERO emojis.
  - ZERO icons (rely entirely on typography, high-quality photography, and spatial layout).
  - NO vertical lines inside cards or grids. Use ample whitespace, subtle background color shifts, and strong typographic hierarchy to separate content.

## 2. Brand Identity
- **Logo:** `assets/LUBE PRESTIGIOUS LOGO.png`
- **Tagline:** "Where Every Drop Carries Prestige"
- **Primary Color (Dark Blue):** `#2d3488` (Use for prominent headings, primary navigation, footer background, and strong contrast elements).
- **Secondary Color (Light Blue):** `#55a8dd` (Use for strategic highlights, active states, and subtle accent blocks).
- **Typography Direction:** A strong, clean sans-serif font family (e.g., Inter, Roboto, or Helvetica Neue) to project industrial reliability minus the clutter.

## 3. Site Architecture & Layout Outline

### A. Header / Navigation
- **Left:** Lube Prestigious Logo
